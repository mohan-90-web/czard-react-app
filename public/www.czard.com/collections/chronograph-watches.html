A NOTE ON HERITAGE UNE NOTE SUR L’HÉRITAGE

WHERE THE PUSHERS COME FROM.

In 1940, Patek Philippe introduced the Ref. 1463, nicknamed “Tasti Tondi,” Italian for round buttons,

for its round, fluted pushers set low into the case band, unlike the protruding pushers of the era. It was also among the first Patek Philippe chronographs with a water-resistant case. Decades on, it remains one of the most studied silhouettes in chronograph history.

Veni's pushers are our own reading of that detail, reinterpreted in rose gold, set into a 38mm case. Not a reissue. An interpretation, offered with the admiration it's earned.
